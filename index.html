Case study · IVF Clinic, Bangkok · SET-listedSEO & AEO10

A listed clinic, absent
from every AI engine.

A fertility clinic on the Stock Exchange of Thailand. 2,000+ patients a year, a 60–65% ICSI success rate, three branches and a seven-doctor team.

The cause was not the clinic. It was four lines in a configuration file.
  • Zero AI answers on any engine
  • English pages serving Thai body copy
  • Core Web Vitals failing at 21/100
Diagnosis
Eight AI crawlers disallowed at robots.txt — a platform default, not a decision.
Consequence
Five clinics recommended. The listed clinic is not among them.
